What we do

Four services for moving from invention to industrial evidence.

The work is hands-on and focused on one question: what needs to be true for an industrial partner to care enough to test, fund, or co-develop?

Technology assessment

Review maturity, claims, proof points, bottlenecks, IP posture, and the next evidence needed for credible industrial conversations.

Industrial matchmaking

Map companies, business units, technical owners, and validation contexts where the technology could solve a measurable problem.

Commercial validation

Run interviews, refine use cases, test willingness to engage, and convert vague interest into a clear next step.

Consortium creation

Shape the partner mix for grants, pilots, applied research programs, venture building, and co-development agreements.

Repeatable process

From research asset to validation sprint.

Frame

Define the technology, claim, use case, and TRL gap in language an industrial team can evaluate.

Prioritize

Select the most promising sectors, companies, and validation environments.

Engage

Run targeted outreach and discovery conversations with technical and business owners.

Convert

Turn positive signals into a pilot, consortium, grant path, venture case, or a disciplined no-go.
