Why this exists

Technical credibility plus venture-building discipline.

TRL Bridge starts from a simple belief: many early technologies do not need a marketplace first. They need sharper positioning, credible validation partners, and a founder-like process for learning where value might exist.

Built for the messy middle between research and industry.

The company is intentionally at the first-step stage. The aim is to use existing deep-tech experience, customer discovery practice, and consortium-building networks to help researchers and startups get out of the lab without pretending every technology is already a venture.

The first sector focus is advanced materials, energy, and manufacturing. Quantum, photonics, and AI-enabled workflows can be added where they connect to real industrial validation needs.

  • 10+ years in deep tech and applied research environments
  • Venture-building experience with HighTechXL-style validation
  • Customer discovery and industrial conversation practice
  • Research network experience across TNO, Jülich, CNRS, and technical groups

Provisional positioning

We help research groups and early deep-tech teams move technologies from TRL 2-3 toward TRL 5-6 by providing industrial matchmaking, commercial validation, and consortium building.
